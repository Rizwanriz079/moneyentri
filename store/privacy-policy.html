FinTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

FinTrack ("the app") is a personal finance app for recording expenses, income, budgets, savings goals, loans and bills. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

Information the app stores

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. This includes transactions, budgets, goals, loans, bills, payments, and the optional name and email on the profile screen. The app has no account system and no server. It does not send your data anywhere.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal or financial data. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device before EMI and bill due dates. Reminders are created and delivered entirely on the device.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show payment reminders.
  • Run at startup: to restore scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibration: for reminder notifications.

Deleting your data

To delete all data, use Profile & settings → Erase all data, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes everything the app stored. Android's device backup may keep a copy of app data if you have backup turned on in system settings.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.
